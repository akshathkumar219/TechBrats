SIH26189: AI-Powered Criminal Network Analysis System
•⁠  ⁠*The Challenge:* Connecting disparate police FIRs, Call Detail Records (CDRs), and suspect associates into a unified criminal syndicate map.
•⁠  ⁠*Architecture & AI Stack:* Fast-Entity Disambiguation using Levenshtein distance + Vector Embeddings $\rightarrow$ Graph Neural Network (GNN via PyG) for link prediction and Louvain community detection $\rightarrow$ Timeline sequence visualizer.
•⁠  ⁠*Hackathon Edge:* Solves a major pain point for law enforcement agencies without requiring complex physical hardware.
